Loot Check — Support

Help & contact

Loot Check identifies an item from a photo, estimates its resale value, and drafts a listing you can post. Here's how to get the most out of it and how to reach us.

Contact us

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email jklwjr@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Tips

  • Get an accurate match. If the result says "best guess," add a close-up photo of the brand logo or label, or type the brand in the detail field, and scan again.
  • Use multiple photos. You can add several photos of the same item (an overall shot plus a close-up) for a better identification.
  • Prices are estimates. The resale value shown is an estimate to guide your pricing, not a guarantee of what an item will sell for.
  • Permissions. The app needs camera and/or photo access to scan items. You can manage these in iOS Settings → Loot Check.
  • Daily limit. There is a daily limit on scans per device to keep the service running smoothly. It resets each day.
